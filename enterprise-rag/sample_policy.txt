ACME Corp Remote Work Policy

1. Introduction
Acme Corp values flexibility and productivity. This remote work policy outlines the guidelines for employees working from locations other than the primary office.

2. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to apply for remote work up to 3 days a week.

3. Equipment
Acme Corp will provide a one-time stipend of $500 for home office setup. Employees are responsible for their own internet connection.

4. Communication
Core hours are from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST. During this time, all remote employees must be online and available via Slack and email.

5. Security
Employees must connect to the corporate VPN when accessing sensitive company data. Public Wi-Fi networks must not be used without the VPN.
